Lap 1/6About

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate at the Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, Noida, and I build AI systems people can actually trust.

That means models that explain their predictions, LLM pipelines whose guardrails are written in code, and agents that ask a human before doing anything that matters. I like taking an idea the whole way: FastAPI backend, React front end, evaluation harness, tests and CI, deployed and live.

I’m drawn to hard problems and comfortable in the mess before they make sense. When something fails, I take it apart, find out why, and rebuild it better. MalariaVision missed 10.4% of infections in its first version; three versions later it missed 3.7%. That loop of fail, learn, improve is the part of engineering I enjoy most.

Lap 3/6Method

Race strategy for AI systems.

The pattern behind most of what I build. The model is fast; the rules keep it on track.

  1. S1

    The model describes

    Images, text, audio and tabular data go to an ML model or LLM that returns structured JSON: observations, not decisions.

  2. S2

    The code decides

    Deterministic rules, guardrails and calibration turn evidence into a verdict. The model can only choose what policy allows.

  3. S3

    A human approves

    Anything consequential, such as a merge, a refund or an offer, waits for a person to sign off.

  4. It ships with evidence

    SHAP explanations, audit trails, evaluation sets and tests travel with every release.

In practice: SaveFlow’s offer guardrails · ChronoTrace’s rule-based verdicts · the Claim Verifier’s calibration layer · Support-to-Ship’s human approvals.
